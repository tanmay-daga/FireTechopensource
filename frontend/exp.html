How it works

From a broken screenshot to a reviewable fix

01

Show the ghost

Upload a screenshot of the broken page. PNG, JPEG or WebP.

02

Point to the haunt

Give the path to your local repository. Secrets, node_modules and build folders are never read.

03

Gemma investigates

Your local model studies the screenshot and the most relevant files, separating evidence from guesses.

04

Review the patch

Get the culprit, a plain-English cause, a Git diff and fixed code. Nothing is applied automatically.
